THE GINKGO COLLECTIVE

Trans collective advocating for transgender autonomy in Pittsburgh, PA


About Us

We are a collective of transgender individuals based in Pittsburgh, PA. We are dedicated to empowering our community with the skills, knowledge, and resources needed to increase our autonomy regarding medical transition, in both formal healthcare settings and independently.
